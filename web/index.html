How to play
  • Click an empty cell, type a word, Enter to play. Space flips direction, Esc cancels, Backspace undoes.
  • Typing across an existing letter reuses it; typing a different letter across a wildcard redefines it (all words must stay real).
  • Click a placed tile to mutate its letter or steal its word — replace the word with a real word of yours; leftovers join your rack (12 max, rest discarded).
  • You can only play after a friend has played. Drag to pan, wheel to zoom.
  • Premiums recur forever: 3×W 2×W 3×L 2×L
  • Scores reset daily; the day's winner earns a ★.
