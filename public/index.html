How to play
  • The first word must cover the ★ start cell. Tap an empty cell, then tap rack tiles (or type) to spell a word across existing letters. ✓/Enter plays it, →↓/Space flips direction, ⌫/Backspace undoes, ✕/Esc cancels.
  • Arrow keys move a board cursor (the view follows): type to start a word there, or press Enter on a tile to steal/mutate it. Arrows also slide a word you're spelling.
  • Add CPU player 🤖 for a computer opponent — hot-seat or online, it moves right after you do.
  • Spelling across a wildcard with a different letter redefines it — every word it belongs to must stay real.
  • Tap a placed tile to mutate its letter (pick from the grid) or steal its word: spell your replacement right over it — its letters are yours to reuse, arrows slide your word along the line, and leftover letters join your rack (12 max, the rest drop back into the bag).
  • ⇄ Exchange swaps rack letters for fresh ones instead of playing, and ⏭ Pass gives up the turn. The day has one 100-tile bag; once it's dry you can 🌙 propose ending the day — others get 2 minutes to agree (unanimous = instant new day) or cancel by playing on. All passing in a row ends the day too.
  • The world is a 120×120 loop — walk off one edge and you come back on the other (words can wrap too). You can only play after a friend has played. Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom. When a word lands off screen the view glides to it; touch the board to take the camera back.
  • Premiums recur forever: 3×W 2×W 3×L 2×L. They only count on letters changed that move.
  • Every day lays out ten fruits within reach — a few cells from the ★ and from the words already down, close enough to chase and far enough to be worth it — and more appear near wherever you just played. Cover one with a letter to eat it: 🍋 two extra letters, 🌶️ a high-scoring letter, 🍒 pick one of seven letters, 🍇 +10 points, 🍌 a fresh rack, 🥝 a wildcard. Their letters are drawn from the day's bag like any other, so a fruit can come up empty once the bag runs low — and the six letters you turn down from a cherry go straight back in.
  • Scores reset daily: fresh bag, fresh racks, the ★ moves, and a new crop of fruit is laid out. The day's winner earns a permanent ★. Two players can't share a name.
  • The player who started the game is the admin 👑. Next to any other player they get ✕ to remove that seat (its letters go back in the bag) and 👑 to hand the admin rights over — handing over is one-way, so the new admin would have to give them back.
