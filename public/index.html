How to play

Play

  • Tap an empty cell, tap or type letters, ✓ plays. ✕ cancel · → direction · ⌫ undo. Or Enter, Space, Backspace, Esc.
  • The first word covers the ★; after that every word must touch what's down.
  • The arrow is guessed from the letters nearby, or points at the nearest word.
  • Nobody plays twice in a row. Games take turns by default; the admin can switch to free-for-all and skip an idler, and eight hours on your turn is skipped for you.

Your letters

  • Drag a tile into any of the twelve slots, gaps included — lay out C _ T to see a play. ⇄ shuffle rearranges the lot.
  • ⇄ Exchange swaps letters back into the bag; ⏭ Pass gives up your turn.
  • One 100-tile bag a day. Once it's dry, 🌙 propose ending the day — two minutes for anyone to object. Everyone passing ends it too.

Other people's words — tap a placed tile

  • Steal: spell your word over theirs along the same line; the leftovers are yours (12 in the rack, then the bag).
  • Overwrite: spell straight across letters already down. Everything must stay real and say something new; what you cover is yours.
  • Mutate: swap one board letter for one of yours and keep the old one. No score, no turn.
  • A wildcard already down can be redefined to fit, so long as its words stay real.

Scoring

  • 3×W 2×W 3×L 2×L recur across the world but pay once, to the first letter to land there. Adding to a word pays face value.
  • A word pays you once a day. Seven tiles at once is +50.
  • Fruit: 🍋 two letters · 🌶️ a big one · 🍒 pick of seven · 🍇 +10 · 🍌 fresh rack · 🥝 wildcard. Ten within reach each day, more where you play.
  • Scores reset daily — fresh bag, fresh racks, the ★ moves — and the day's winner keeps a ★.

Getting around

  • A 448×448 loop. Drag to pan, pinch or ＋ － to zoom, ⌖ for the last word — which always wears a halo. Arrow keys move a cursor.
  • 👤 sign in and your games follow you to any device; 🔔 tells you when it's your turn. 🎲 lists your games, ● for the ones waiting on you.
  • Whoever started the game is admin 👑 and can remove players or hand the crown on. Setup folds away under Players & setup once play starts.
