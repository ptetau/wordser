How to play
  • Spelling a word. Tap an empty cell, then tap rack tiles (or type) to spell across existing letters. The four buttons under Placing are ✕ cancel, → dir, ⌫ undo and ✓ play — ✓ shows what the word will score, and greys out with the reason when the word won't do. Keys: Enter plays, Space flips direction, Backspace undoes, Esc cancels.
  • The direction is guessed from the letters around the cell you tapped: a gap between two tiles runs along them, a tile above or below starts a downward hook, and a corner heads for the open side. With nothing touching, it points at the nearest word — along the axis that word lies on, so you can spell and then slide into it with the arrow keys. Change it any time with → dir or Space.
  • The first word of a game must cover the ★ start cell; after that every word must touch what's already down.
  • 👤 Your account (top right) is where you sign in or make an account — then your games follow you to any device, and opening a game link on your phone puts you back in your own seat. The same menu shows your profile and can change your passphrase.
  • 🎲 Your games lists every table you're at, with whose move it is; the ones waiting on you carry a ● and the button a badge. Tap one to go there.
  • 🔔 Tell me when it's my turn, in the account menu, sends a notification when a game is yours to play — including games in other tabs. Never for the game you're looking at, and once per turn at most. The tab title shows a ● either way.
  • Games take turns by default, in seat order; the admin can switch the table to free-for-all under Players & setup. Either way nobody plays twice in a row. The admin can skip a player who is holding things up, and anyone who sits on their turn for eight hours is passed by.
  • Write over a word by spelling straight across it: every word it touches must stay real, it has to say something new, and the letters you cover are yours. Letters that already fit cost nothing.
  • A word pays you once a day — swapping a letter back and forth to re-score it earns nothing the second time.
  • Whose turn it is is written at the top of Actions. Nobody plays twice in a row — after your move you wait for somebody else, even if you're alone at the table, where the way on is Add CPU player 🤖 or a friend joining. Mutating, picking a cherry's letter and voting on the end of the day aren't turns. Whoever turns up, friend or robot, is announced to everyone already playing.
  • Arrow keys move a board cursor (the view follows): type to start a word there, or press Enter on a tile to steal/mutate it. Arrows also slide a word you're spelling.
  • The tray has twelve slots, and a tile can go in any of them — including the empty ones. Drag a letter aside to leave a gap and lay out C _ T to see the play before you make it; the others slide aside to make room. It works mid-word too, and a letter you have already put on the board leaves its slot open. ⇄ shuffle rearranges the lot.
  • Tap a placed tile for the things you can do to it: steal its word — spell your replacement right over it, its letters are free, arrows slide your word along the line, and leftovers join your rack (12 max, the rest drop back into the bag) — or mutate one letter. ✕ Never mind backs out.
  • Mutating is a trade, not a play. Swap one of your letters for a letter on the board and you keep the one you prise off — but it scores nothing and it doesn't use your turn. It's how you fix a hopeless rack from the board when the bag is dry; play the word it sets up afterwards.
  • Spelling across a wildcard with a different letter redefines it — every word it belongs to must stay real.
  • ⇄ Exchange swaps rack letters for fresh ones instead of playing, and ⏭ Pass gives up the turn. While you're exchanging or stealing, the rack is outlined so you know which mode you're in. The day has one 100-tile bag; once it's dry you can 🌙 propose ending the day — others get 2 minutes to agree (unanimous = instant new day) or cancel by playing on. All passing in a row ends the day too.
  • Getting around. The world is a 448×448 loop — walk off one edge and you come back on the other, and words can wrap too. Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, or use ＋ － on the board. When a word lands off screen the view glides to it; touch the board to take the camera back, and ⌖ returns you to the last word played. The last word always wears a halo — light around the tiles, never over the letters — so somebody else's move is obvious at a glance.
  • Premiums recur forever across the world — 3×W 2×W 3×L 2×L — but each square pays only once, to the first letter that lands on it. Adding to a word therefore scores face value for the letters already down, and rewriting an old word never re-mines its bonuses. Fresh ground is where the points are.
  • Every day lays out ten fruits within reach — a few cells from the ★ and from the words already down, close enough to chase and far enough to be worth it — and more appear near wherever you just played. Cover one with a letter to eat it: lemon 🍋 two extra letters, chilli 🌶️ a high-scoring letter, cherry 🍒 pick one of seven letters, grape 🍇 +10 points, banana 🍌 a fresh rack, kiwi 🥝 a wildcard. Each one glints into place when it appears. Their letters are drawn from the day's bag like any other, so a fruit can come up empty once the bag runs low — and the six letters you turn down from a cherry go straight back in.
  • Scores reset daily: fresh bag, fresh racks, the ★ moves, and a new crop of fruit is laid out. The day's winner earns a permanent ★. Two players can't share a name.
  • The player who started the game is the admin 👑. Next to any other player they get ✕ remove for that seat (its letters go back in the bag) and 👑 admin to hand the rights over — handing over is one-way, so the new admin would have to give them back.
  • New here? Controls you haven't used yet catch the light for a moment when they're the thing to press.
  • Once play starts, setup folds itself away under Players & setup — that's where adding players, inviting friends, the share link and End the day early ★ live. Open it any time; it stays open until you close it.
