How to play
  • Tap an empty cell, then tap rack tiles (or type) to spell a word across existing letters. ✓/Enter plays it, →↓/Space flips direction, ⌫/Backspace undoes, ✕/Esc cancels.
  • Spelling across a wildcard with a different letter redefines it — every word it belongs to must stay real.
  • Tap a placed tile to mutate its letter or steal its word: replace the word with your own; leftover letters join your rack (12 max, rest discarded).
  • You can only play after a friend has played. Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • Premiums recur forever: 3×W 2×W 3×L 2×L. They only count on letters changed that move.
  • Bonus fruits appear near the action — cover one with a letter to eat it: 🍋 two extra letters, 🌶️ a high-scoring letter, 🍒 pick one letter from a choice of seven.
  • Scores reset daily; the day's winner earns a ★.
