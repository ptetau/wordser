How to play
  • Spell a word across the ★ — tap a cell and type, or drag tiles from your rack. Every word after that builds on what's down.
  • Drop a tile on a letter already played to write over it: ⇄ swap keeps the letters you prise off, ▦ stack keeps the points.
  • 3×W 2×W 3×L 2×L pay the first letter to land there. A word pays you once a day. Fruit are a surprise — eat them.
  • Each day: fresh bag, fresh racks, a new ★ on clean ground. Yesterday's words fade out of bounds until you bridge back out to them.
  • The rest the game will teach you — every move is priced before you commit, and anything on screen says what it is if you hover or hold it.
