How to play

Play

  • Tap an empty cell, tap or type letters, ✓ plays. ✕ cancel · → direction · ⌫ undo. Or Enter, Space, Backspace, Esc.
  • Letters go down in any order. Type along the line, or drop tiles wherever you like — the ends first, the middle after. Tapping the board moves the cursor and leaves the letters where you put them; tap one of your own to take it back.
  • A word may run straight over letters already down. Ones that already say what you are typing come along for free; the rest are written over, which spends a tile and posts the old letter back to the bag. Type HUMAN across MEN — or lay out H U _ A _ and drop the three — and the M and N are reused, the E replaced. At least one letter must land on empty ground: a word has to go somewhere new.
  • Islands. Each day opens its own island on a fresh ★, and every play must touch the island you are on. Older days' words lie faded on the board, out of bounds — until somebody bridges out to them with a line of words, and then the two are one island and all of it is in play again. The new ★ is never further from the old words than a quarter of a bag of letters can span.
  • The arrow is guessed from the letters nearby, or points at the nearest word.
  • Nobody plays twice in a row. Games take turns by default; the admin can switch to free-for-all, and eight hours on your turn is skipped for you. A seat with no letters left passes itself.
  • Holding things up? Each seat says how long it has been quiet. The one with the turn carries ⏭ for the admin to move play on, and every seat carries 💤 to have its turns passed until whoever sits there comes back.
  • Busy today? Tick 💤 I'm busy — pass my turns under ⚙ table rules and your turns pass themselves until you come back; the banner has an I'm back button whenever you want them again. If everybody is busy the game simply waits.
  • When somebody joins a game already going, the table is offered a fresh start — and gets to choose who leads off.
  • Playing to a number of words? Set one under ⚙ table rules and the corner of the board counts down. The game ends on the last word, the top score wins, and the final table goes up under Actions — where the admin picks ★ new day, which keeps the board, or ↺ new game, which starts from nothing.
  • 🏳️ Forfeit under Actions gives up: your letters go back in the bag and your seat closes. If you were the last opponent, the other player wins.

Your letters

  • Drag a tile into any of the twelve slots, gaps included — lay out C _ T to see a play. ⇄ shuffle rearranges the lot. Drag one onto the board to play it, or onto a letter already down to write over it.
  • ⇄ Exchange swaps letters back into the bag; ⏭ Pass gives up your turn.
  • One 100-tile bag a day to play from — the fruit have a second hundred of their own, so what they hand out never comes off the table. Once the playing bag is dry, 🌙 propose ending the day — two minutes for anyone to object. Everyone passing ends it too.

Swapping and stacking — with no word on the go, drag a tile onto a letter

  • Nothing being spelled? Then a tile dropped on a letter starts a raid on the board instead. Keep going: each letter after the first must land in a word one of the others already touches, so a turn is one reach, not errands in three places.
  • Every word you touch must still be real. Either way it takes your turn, and you choose how it settles:
  • ⇄ Swap — you keep every letter you prise off, and score nothing.
  • ▦ Stack — the words you have rewritten pay you, and the letters you wrote over go into the bag for somebody else. Premiums are long spent, and a word still pays you only once a day.
  • A wildcard already down can be redefined to fit, so long as its words stay real.

Scoring

  • 3×W 2×W 3×L 2×L recur across the world but pay once, to the first letter to land there. Adding to a word pays face value.
  • A word pays you once a day. Seven tiles at once is +50, and laying out a full rack in one word doubles it.
  • Cover a fruit with a letter to eat it. Ten are laid out within reach each day, and more appear where you play — what each one gives is for you to find out.
  • Scores reset daily — fresh bag, fresh racks, the ★ moves to clean ground and everyone's view goes with it. The day's winner keeps a ★, and the last five days are listed under Last five days. Hover a name for their record.

Getting around

  • A 448×448 loop. Drag to pan, pinch or ＋ － to zoom, ⌖ for the last word — which always wears a halo. Arrow keys move a cursor.
  • 👤 sign in and your games follow you to any device; 🔔 tells you when it's your turn. 🎲 lists your games, ● for the ones waiting on you.
  • Whoever started the game is admin 👑 and can remove players or hand the crown on. Setup folds away under ⚙ table rules once play starts.
